## Across oceans on unconventional vessels

Earlier this year, I visited Oslo and went to the Kon-Tiki Museum to learn more about Thor Heyerdahl's expeditions.

I first learnt about this Norwegian explorer from my parents—they had a book about the Kon-Tiki expedition. Funnily enough, I never read it. However, at the start of the year, I picked up another of his books, Aku-Aku, at a book fair at the botanical gardens. I was fascinated by his account of the expedition and decided I wouldn't miss the museum if I ever got the chance to visit Oslo—so I did.

Above is a map showing approximations of his various expeditions across a variety of vessels. I still can't wrap my head around how recent this all was, or how brave and adventurous he and his team were to take on these voyages.

Routes are approximate GeoJSON reconstructions based on historical expedition records and Kon-Tiki Museum documentation.
